About Me

Where data meets story

I'm a Data Analyst with a passion for uncovering the narratives hidden inside numbers. My work bridges the gap between raw, messy datasets and clear, confident business decisions.

Over the years I've worked across retail, finance, and operations — building dashboards that get used, writing queries that get trusted, and presenting findings that actually change minds.

I believe great analysis isn't just about accuracy — it's about clarity. The best insight in the world is worthless if it doesn't communicate. That's why storytelling sits at the heart of everything I do.

"Data without a story is noise. My goal is to turn noise into signal — and signal into strategy."

Methodology

How I work

01

Frame the right question

Before touching data, I work with stakeholders to define the real business question. Bad framing is the #1 cause of useless analysis.

02

Understand the data deeply

Thorough EDA, quality checks, and documentation. I never skip the boring parts — they're where the surprises live.

03

Build for reproducibility

Clean, commented code. Version-controlled workflows. Notebooks anyone can re-run six months later without asking me questions.

04

Communicate the story

Every analysis ends with a clear narrative — what happened, why it matters, and what to do about it. No jargon, no buried lede.
